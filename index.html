Meet the maker

A small, local studio with a big heart for your memories.

Feven' Prints started with a simple belief: the photos we love deserve better than being lost on a phone. So Feven turned her eye for detail into a little print studio — printing and finishing every order by hand, right here in Addis Ababa.
